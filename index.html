GuidelineATS 2026 — Noninvasive respiratory support (post-extubation, PICO 4)
  • Suggests HFNC or facemask NIV over standard oxygen after extubation in the ICU (conditional, moderate certainty).
  • For patients at high risk of extubation failure, suggests facemask NIV over standard oxygen or HFNC (conditional, moderate certainty).
  • Patients at very low risk may be extubated to standard low-flow oxygen or room air.
  • Applies to prophylactic use after planned extubation, not rescue therapy. NIV with HFNC breaks is described as a practical way to deliver NIV; combined strategies are not formally recommended.

The guideline supports a risk-based approach. It does not define high risk the way ARISE does — see the RISK review notes for where thresholds differ.

C. Patient outcomes
Current outcome framework
  • Reintubation within 48 hours of planned extubation
  • Death within 48 hours
  • Unplanned rescue NIV / HFNC
  • Individual components of the exploratory deterioration outcome
Future research consideration
  • Time from successful SBT to extubation
  • Mechanical ventilation duration
  • ICU length of stay
  • Repeated SBT requirements
  • Subsequent support escalation
Research governance
  • The current concordance study stays aligned with its approved methodology (NHG DSRB / ECOS 2026-1209-APP1).
  • No Tab 4 variable is added to the study dataset. Concordance definitions, eligibility and outcome measures are unchanged.
  • Anything proposed here is a future research consideration and needs study-team and institutional approval.
